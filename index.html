Ravaewyn Privacy Policy

Ravaewyn is made by Blackmoon Interactive. This policy covers the Ravaewyn app for Android, including its beta (test) versions. Last updated: November 17, 2026.

The short version

Ravaewyn has no account, no ads and no tracking. In the beta, the questions you ask are recorded so the readings can be improved. Your birthday and your name stay on your phone. Crash and speed information from the beta is sent too, as agreed on the first screen.

What the app collects

  • Questions you type. The text of each question, with the topic and reading level used. In the beta this is recorded for everyone and cannot be turned off, and the app tells you so on its first screen. No name, email or account is attached.
  • Phone details. Your phone model, Android version and app version, and a random install number made by the app. The install number is not linked to you and is replaced if you reinstall.
  • Speed information. Frame rate, memory use and graphics details, sent in the beta, and when you press the speed test button in the app.
  • Crash and diagnostic reports. What the app was doing when something went wrong, sent in the beta. Google Play may also give us crash reports for apps it distributes.
  • Things you choose to send. Suggestions and feedback you write in the app, and name requests.

What stays on your phone

Your birthday, your name, your settings, which reading levels are unlocked, and the history that stops Ravaewyn repeating herself stay on your phone. They are never sent to us.

What we do not collect

No account, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or advertising ID. The app contains no advertising and no third-party tracking, and we do not sell your data.

How the information is used

Only to improve the readings, fix problems and understand how well the app runs on different phones.

How it is sent and who sees it

Information the app sends is transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to the developer through the ntfy.sh notification service, which carries the message to us. It is not shared with anyone else and is not used for advertising.

Keeping and deleting

We keep these reports only as long as we need them to improve the app. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone. Because nothing identifies you, we cannot look up your questions afterwards by name. If you want something removed, write to us at the address below and describe it, for example by giving the date and a few words of the question.

Children

Ravaewyn is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Entertainment only

Ravaewyn's readings and horoscopes are for entertainment and reflection. They are not medical, legal, financial or any other professional advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and in the app.
